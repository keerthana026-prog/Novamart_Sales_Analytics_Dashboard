# NovaMart Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![NovaMart Sales Analytics Dashboard](DASHBOARD%20SC.png)

## 🎯 Project Overview

NovaMart is a fictional retail business used for this project. The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful business information to help understand revenue, profit, sales trends, and regional performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Excel formulas and KPI calculations
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive slicers and filtering
- Data visualization and business reporting

## ✨ Key Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales Revenue, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Overall Profit Margin.
- **Regional Analysis:** Compare sales performance across Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Mumbai.
- **Category Analysis:** Compare revenue across product categories.
- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Explore sales revenue patterns across 2025 and 2026.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Use slicers to explore the dashboard by region and category.

## 📌 Key Metrics

- **Total Sales Revenue:** 4,687,500
- **Total Profit:** 1,241,347.5
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 2,193
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 26.48%

*Note: The dataset is simulated for learning and portfolio purposes. These figures do not represent actual business results.*
